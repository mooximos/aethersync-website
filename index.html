A $875 camera unit for a traffic-signal pole

Four cameras on one corner signal pole, one aimed down each approach at its stop line and queue. Inside a weatherproof box, a Raspberry Pi 5 with a 13 TOPS AI accelerator detects vehicles on the device. It plugs into an outlet on the pole through a sealed 12 V adapter, so 120 V never enters the box, and costs about $875 to build, cellular link included.
